Section 03

How We Stay Safe

Containment

Sandboxed by Design

  • Isolated sandbox — ESLint installed in .blitzy-eslint-sandbox/.
  • No lockfile drift — host manifests are read-only.
  • No CI integration — existing workflows unchanged.
  • Biome remains canonical — this scan augments, never replaces.
