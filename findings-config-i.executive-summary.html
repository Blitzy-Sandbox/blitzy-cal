Key Takeaway

One scan. Three deliverables.

  • 263 findings normalized into the 5-field comparison schema; PASSED the default quality gate; valid JSON, one line, UTF-8.
  • One high-leverage CRITICAL in packages/lib/crypto.ts — CWE-327, secure mode & padding. Everything else is structural or test-hygiene.
  • 40 documented decisions — auth-token, Node-heap, exclusions, CWE tiers — covering every deviation from a literal reading of the directives.
